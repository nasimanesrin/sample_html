Review of the Movie

Train to Busan (2016) is one of the most intense and emotional zombie thrillers ever made. Directed by Yeon Sang-ho, the film follows a father and his young daughter on a train from Seoul to Busan—right as a deadly zombie outbreak explodes across South Korea. What begins as a normal journey quickly turns into a desperate fight for survival. The movie stands out because it combines action, emotion, and social themes beautifully. The zombies are fast, terrifying, and relentless, but the real strength of the film is its characters. Each passenger has a different personality and motive, and the story shows both the best and worst sides of humanity during a crisis. Gong Yoo delivers a powerful performance as a father learning to become more caring, while Kim Su-an, playing his daughter, gives one of the most memorable child performances in Korean cinema. The emotional moments hit hard, especially in the final scenes. The film also carries a deeper message about selfishness vs. sacrifice, making it more meaningful than a typical zombie movie. Combined with fast pacing, thrilling action scenes, and strong character arcs, Train to Busan becomes an unforgettable experience.
